Device Lab how XR hardware works

Eight devices you will use or meet when building digital twins, rebuilt at real size from published specifications. Each page runs a small physical model live: change the slider, read the number, then explode, cut away or x-ray the device, and press the red button to see what breaks it.

How to use it in class

  1. Open a device on a laptop, predict the number before you move the slider, then check.
  2. Put on a Quest 3, open the same page in the Meta Quest Browser and press Enter MR. Hold the real device next to the virtual one.
  3. Link back to the book lesson: stereo → ZED 2i, splats → X5, LiDAR → Pro3, bidirectional data → Lighthouse and ZED.
